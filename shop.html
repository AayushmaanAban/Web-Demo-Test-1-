How a MOON piece is made

Small runs, altered by hand, finished by the wearer.

Runs are capped because the units are small. Bottoms are cut locally, tops are finished with details that let you adjust the silhouette yourself — velcro side tabs, waist straps, a button system that turns a trouser into a jort.

Convertible construction and surface detail are part of the design language, alongside beadwork, tiger and leopard references and saree upcycling.
