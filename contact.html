Shipping, sizing, care and returns.

Orders are dispatched from the MOON studio in India within 3–5 working days. Free shipping across India on orders over ₹2,999.

International shipping is quoted per order while the label scales — write to the studio with your city.

Placeholder copy for the theme demo.

MOON bottoms and tops are sized S–L and cut unisex unless stated. The label builds for a relaxed silhouette, so between sizes take the larger one.

Boot Leg ’72 and ’73 sit high on the waist with a flare from the knee. Toji Pants run baggy with a cuffed ankle. Lapze Cap is one size with an adjustable fit.

14-day exchange on unworn pieces with tags attached. Altered and made-to-order pieces are final sale.

Cold wash, inside out, mild detergent, line dry in shade. Denim: wash sparingly to keep the depth of colour. Linen leopard print: gentle hand wash.

Tracking is emailed when the piece leaves the studio. In this demo build, orders are simulated and nothing ships.

Several silhouettes can be cut to your measurements, and finished pieces can be altered at the studio. Tell us the piece and the measurement you need.
