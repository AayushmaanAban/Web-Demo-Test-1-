The meaning

MOON is an emotion, not a planet.

MOON is not astronomical or space-related. MOON is an emotion — the feeling of being different from everyone else. “Alien-like” means feeling different, not outer space.

Why this matters for the store: nothing in the experience should reach for cosmic imagery. The “alien” feeling is expressed through silhouette, proportion and restraint — clothes that look strange on the hanger and obvious on the body.

Positioning

Indian soul × global fashion language.

A contemporary Indian streetwear label transforming heritage, craftsmanship and emotion into experimental luxury silhouettes.

Brand DNA

Core idea, personality, hierarchy.

  • Core idea. Indian soul × global fashion language.
  • Personality. Minimal, experimental, premium, youthful, emotional, distinctive, slightly strange and deeply rooted in India.
  • Design hierarchy. Silhouette > graphics. Fit, proportion, construction, fabric and details should make the garment recognisable.
  • Emotional proposition. MOON gives people a way to express individuality — to feel unlike everyone else without needing to explain it.
Design language

Experimental silhouette, unconventional construction.

Bootcut Bell-bottom Baggy trousers Jorts Convertible garments Unconventional construction Surface detail Beadwork Tiger / leopard references Saree upcycling

Creative reference: Balenciaga × India; Sabyasachi × Balenciaga — not as copies, but as a collision of global luxury language with Indian cultural depth.

Indian identity

Felt, not shouted.

Indian heritage should be felt rather than shouted. Use textiles, craft, makers, cultural references and techniques through a modern global lens — never simply “Indian prints on Western clothes.”

Craft & slow fashion

The maker becomes part of the product story.

Limited collections, smaller quantities, local tailoring, thoughtful construction and upcycling. Bootleg ’72 — the 72-year-old tailor concept — represents this human side of MOON.

GSM denim

“Fifty years of cutting, and the flare still has to fall exactly right.”

Hand-stitched, not just finished by hand. Boot Leg ’72 comes off one local tailor’s table — single-needle seams, a hand bar-tack at every belt loop, hems turned by hand. Five decades of cutting is the only way that standard survives a run this small.

Product philosophy

Build a wardrobe, not a logo collection.

  • Bottoms and silhouettes can become signature categories.
  • Pieces should have a reason to exist: unusual shape, transformability, craft, material story or emotional meaning.
The customer

Fashion-conscious, 18–25, and unwilling to look like everyone else.

Values individuality, unusual silhouettes, street culture, exclusivity and meaning. Working profile: roughly ₹1.5–4.5L salary range.

“I don’t want to look like everyone else. I want my clothes to say something about me.”

Mindset

MOON world

A culture around fashion × music × art × sport × youth × Indian identity.

The founder journey is part of the brand: ambitious, raw, personal and community-driven. The audience should feel like they are watching and helping build MOON.
